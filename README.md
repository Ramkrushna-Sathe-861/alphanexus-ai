# AlphaNexus AI

## AI-Powered Trading Research & Market Intelligence Platform

AlphaNexus AI is an AI-powered trading research and market intelligence platform designed to help traders and investment analysts investigate market-related questions.

The platform uses an intelligent AI agent to understand research questions, create a research plan, select appropriate tools, gather information from multiple data sources, analyze the findings, and generate structured market intelligence reports.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Traders and investment analysts often need to manually collect information from multiple sources before making research decisions.

This information may come from:

- Historical market data
- Financial data
- Company information
- Research documents
- External market information

Manually searching, combining, and analyzing this information can be time-consuming.
